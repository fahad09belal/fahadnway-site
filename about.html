My Journey

Before November 2023, I was entirely outside the world of technology. I didn't have a background in coding or computer science, but I had an intense, growing curiosity about how things worked under the hood. That curiosity pushed me to download Termux on an Android device. Instead of relying on heavy desktop IDEs or guided tutorials, I started from absolute scratch in a raw, terminal-based environment. It was a baptism by fire into the command line, and building small, experimental programs quickly turned from a passing interest into an obsession.

That experimentation culminated in my first true milestone: PhalqonOS. I designed a live Semantic Block Encoding System operating entirely through a Bash script. Building a custom cipher and an automated decoding engine in a mobile terminal was my proving ground. It was the exact moment I transitioned from being a consumer of technology to a creator. PhalqonOS taught me system architecture, computational logic, and the symbolic philosophy of control—proving that you don't need a high-end setup to build complex, functional software.

The mechanics I learned building PhalqonOS didn't just sit in a vacuum; they became the architectural foundation for everything that followed. The problem-solving skills developed in that first Bash script scaled up, directly paving the way for larger, overarching digital ecosystems like F.A.H.A.D and the aggressively styled Project !Froyo¡. I learned how to connect disparate systems, manage logic flow, and build environments that carry a distinct, street-level identity.
